# Mission Reflection — Multi-Cloud Explorer

## Impressions & Key Learnings
**Which cloud platform impressed you the most?**
GCP impressed me most. Its clean interface, developer experience, and leadership in Kubernetes and AI feel modern and forward-looking. GCP’s global network and open-source approach give it a distinct technical edge despite AWS’s larger catalogue.

**What similarities exist among AWS, Azure, and GCP?**
All three offer the same fundamental building blocks: virtual machines, object storage, VPC networking, managed databases, and container orchestration. They share pay-as-you-go, regions/AZs for reliability, IAM security, and auto-scaling. The core cloud model is identical — differences lie in ecosystem, pricing, and specialty services.

**How do business requirements influence cloud platform selection?**
Technical features alone never decide the choice. Budget, existing software, compliance, team skills, and growth plans determine the winner. A Microsoft shop picks Azure; an AI lab picks GCP; a broad enterprise picks AWS. The best cloud is the one that solves the client’s specific problems.

**What did you learn from acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant?**
I learned to avoid "most popular" bias and instead match requirements to strengths. Asking *what* and *who* before *which* prevents costly mistakes. Cloud consulting is about business value, not just technology.

**How has your GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio improved after completing this mission?**
This mission added structured research, comparison tables, and decision matrices to my portfolio. I now maintain consistent Markdown formatting, meaningful commits, and evidence files — building a professional resource I can reference and demonstrate to future employers.
